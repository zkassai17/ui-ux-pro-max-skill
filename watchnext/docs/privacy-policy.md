This policy explains what information the watchnext mobile app ("watchnext", "we", "us") collects, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using watchnext you agree to this policy.

## Who we are

watchnext is a social app for tracking movies and TV shows, getting personalized recommendations, and deciding what to watch with friends.

## Information we collect

We only collect what's needed to run the app:

- **Account information** — your email address and password (the password is handled by our authentication provider and is never visible to us).
- **Profile information** — your first name, last name, username, and an auto-generated friend code.
- **Your activity** — the movies and TV shows you add to your library, their status (Want / Watching / Watched), your personal star ratings, and titles you mark "Not interested."
- **Social information** — your friend connections, recommendations you send or receive, and any block or report actions you take.
- **Preferences** — your chosen app language, default library tab, and recommendation settings.
- **Limited device information** — your device's language setting, used to pick a default app language.

We do **not** collect your contacts, precise location, photos, or advertising identifiers. We do **not** show ads. We do **not** sell your personal information.

## How we use your information

- To create and operate your account.
- To save your library, ratings, and preferences across sessions and devices.
- To generate your personalized "Movies/Shows for you" recommendations and "Watch together" group picks.
- To power social features (friends, recommendations, taste-match).
- To keep the service safe (reviewing reports, honoring blocks).

## Third-party services

watchnext relies on two third-party services:

- **Supabase** — hosts our database and authentication. Your account and app data are stored on Supabase's infrastructure. See Supabase's privacy practices for details on how they process data on our behalf.
- **The Movie Database (TMDB)** — provides movie and TV metadata (titles, posters, genres, where-to-watch). When the app fetches title information it sends the title identifier and your selected content-language to TMDB; it does **not** send your name, email, or account data. This product uses the TMDB API but is not endorsed or certified by TMDB.

We do not share your personal information with any other third parties except as required by law.

## Data retention and deletion

We keep your information for as long as your account exists. **You can permanently delete your account at any time** from **Settings → Delete account**. Deleting your account permanently removes your profile, library, ratings, friends, recommendations, and all associated data. This action cannot be undone.

## Security

Data is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Access to your data is restricted by row-level security so that, in general, only you and the friends you choose can see your information.

## Children's privacy

watchnext is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will delete it.

## Your choices

- Edit your name, username, and preferences in Settings.
- Export your data as a CSV from Settings.
- Block or report other users from their profile.
- Delete your account at any time.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Effective date" above.
